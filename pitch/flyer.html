NostosHackUTA 2026

Weather, energy, commodity and shipping data on one live globe

Nostos pulls NOAA forecasts, the US power grid, 35 commodity futures, live ships and prediction markets into one database, each row stamped with when it was known, then serves it on a globe, an API and 47 MCP tools.

Weather 1.57 °F

Daily-high forecasts for Kalshi's 20 cities

  • Mean error one day ahead, against 2.31 °F for the NWS blend.
  • Five models, bias-corrected and reweighted every night.
  • Leakage-tested: future data cannot move a forecast.
Energy 83

Grid respondents, hourly, from EIA-930

  • Demand, forecast and fuel mix, plus 81 sub-zones.
  • 146 airport stations stand for each operator's load.
  • Weather at the fuel hubs: Cushing, Henry Hub, Permian, Haynesville.
Commodities 35

Futures from crude oil to cattle

  • Oil, gas, metals, crops, livestock, each with its pricing point.
  • Beside the price: FRED spot, CFTC positions, ETFs, Kalshi odds.
  • A Gemini note per commodity and country, from those sources only.
Shipping 92

Ports in a weather-aware voyage router

  • Time-aware A* prices the sea at the hour the ship arrives.
  • Speed model trained on real AIS fixes, with a 90% ETA band.
  • Live AIS vessels, about 495 tide stations and SCFI freight rates.
