NostosWeather

Five models, corrected nightly, scored without peeking

  1. Pull ECMWF, GFS, ICON, GEM and JMA

    Hourly runs from the Open-Meteo Previous Runs API, 1 to 3 days ahead.

  2. Remove each model's recent bias

    Its average error over the last 30 days, from reports already published.

  3. Weight by recent accuracy

    Inverse error weights with a 0.5 °F floor, issued at 23:00 local.

  1. Late reports wait their turn

    19 corrected reports join the bias only from the moment they were published.

  2. Future data cannot move it

    Adding 500 °F to every unpublished observation leaves the forecast unchanged.

  3. The rival gets the same fix

    Corrected NBM reaches 1.92 °F. The blend still wins by 0.32 to 0.37 °F.

NostosEnergy

Every US grid operator, every hour

  • 83 EIA-930 respondents: 69 balancing authorities, 13 regions and the US48 total.
  • 81 sub-zones: ERCOT weather zones, PJM utilities, NYISO A to K, CAISO utilities.
  • Five hourly series: demand, day-ahead forecast, net generation, interchange and generation by fuel.
  • 146 airport stations stand for each operator's load, weighted by metro population.
  • History from February 2024, refreshed every hour.

NostosEnergy

Global forecasts for 233 cities, fetched by the byte

1 GB

downloaded per run, instead of 36 GB

Each GFS step is a 500 MB file with 740 fields. Nostos reads its index and pulls only the 16 fields it needs with HTTP range requests, so a 72-hour run takes one to two minutes.

NostosPlatform

47 MCP tools over one TimescaleDB

  • Ingest: one service schedules every feed, retries into a dead-letter queue and checks freshness.
  • Store: Tiger TimescaleDB hypertables, each row stamped with when it was known.
  • Serve: FastAPI for the React globe, FastMCP for any agent at /mcp.
  • Host: Docker Compose on Vultr Cloud Compute, models in Vultr Object Storage, Cloudflare Tunnel in front.

NostosLimits

What we have not proven yet

One season of weather tests

The daily-high test covers 60 days of early fall at 20 big-city stations. Winter cold snaps are not in it.

Settlement source

Kalshi settles on The Weather Company's reading of the climate report, which can differ after a late correction.

Ships near Denmark

The speed model's real AIS rows come from the Danish archive. Open-ocean storms lean on the physics prior.

Forecast horizon

Marine forecasts end near 9 days. Longer voyages hold the last value and say how much was beyond it.
